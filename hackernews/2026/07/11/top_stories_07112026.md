<p class='hn-text-en'>Researchers analyze billions of sketches to show that concept representations vary across cultures, challenging universal assumptions in AI and cognitive science. The findings imply that AI systems trained on a single cultural dataset may misinterpret concepts in diverse user groups.</p>
<p class='hn-text-zh'>研究者对数十亿幅素描进行分析，揭示人类概念在不同文化中存在显著差异，质疑AI与认知科学中的普遍性假设。结果意味着以单一文化数据集训练的AI系统在不同群体中的概念理解可能存在偏差。</p>
