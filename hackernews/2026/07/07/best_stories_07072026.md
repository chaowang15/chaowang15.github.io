<p class='hn-text-en'>Kani brings model checking to Rust, enabling exhaustive verification of safety properties beyond conventional tests. It integrates with Rust tooling and uses an LLVM backend to explore possible states, catching concurrency and memory-safety issues early. This lowers barriers to formal verification for systems programming.</p>
<p class='hn-text-zh'>Kani 将模型检查引入 Rust，能够对安全属性进行穷举验证，超越传统测试的覆盖面。它与 Rust 工具链集成，借助 LLVM 后端遍历潜在状态，提前发现并发与内存安全问题。这将降低系统编程中的形式化验证门槛。</p>
