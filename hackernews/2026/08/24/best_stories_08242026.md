<p class='hn-text-en'>Tests show coconut-oil-based jet fuel delivering comparable efficiency to kerosene in engines, signaling a potential sustainable alternative for aviation fuel. However, questions remain about scalability, supply chains, and lifecycle emissions. If scalable, natural-feedstock fuels could diversify aviation fuel options.</p>
<p class='hn-text-zh'>发动机测试显示以椰子油为基础的喷气燃料在效率上与煤油相当，提示其可能成为航空燃料的可持续替代方案。但关于规模化、供应链和全生命周期排放等仍有待解决；若可规模化，天然原料燃料能为航空燃料选项带来更多样性。</p>
