# 2026-11-02

## Expandable digest details

- Weekly and monthly digest cards now keep their stored preview image and
  bilingual summaries hidden behind a per-card toggle.
- Digest headlines now open the original article directly instead of detouring
  through the corresponding daily page.
- Details reuse existing daily JSON, so digest generation adds no model or
  article-fetching costs. Missing images produce no placeholder.
- Re-rendered every existing monthly and weekly digest from its saved JSON so
  all historical digest pages use the same expandable layout without changing
  Daily pages or historical digest data.
- Removed the per-card corner button. Digest cards now expand or collapse by
  clicking the compact title/score surface; links and expanded content retain
  their normal behavior.
- Unified Daily, Weekly, and Monthly story cards. All now start expanded and
  use the title/score surface to collapse or reopen details; Daily pages retain
  their existing stories, scores, order, and links.
- Added browser-local story favorites. A star control on each card saves its
  title, summaries, image URL, metadata, and original link to `localStorage`;
  `/hackernews/favorites/` displays the saved list without accounts or a server.
