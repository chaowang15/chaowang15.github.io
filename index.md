## About Me

I am a Machine Learning Engineer on the **Avatar AI** team at [Roblox](https://corp.roblox.com/), where I work on **3D generative AI** for avatars, including avatar generation, rigging, and animation.

Previously, at [Meta Reality Labs](https://about.meta.com/realitylabs/), I worked on automatic 3D face rigging and on audiovisual motion models and video generation for real-time interactive avatars. Before that, in the Intelligent Creation Lab at [ByteDance / TikTok](https://www.bytedance.com/en/), I worked on portrait animation, talking-head and human video generation, and real-time facial animation for TikTok Avatars. Earlier, I worked on large-scale 3D scene reconstruction at [Matterport](https://matterport.com/discover).

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [University of Texas at Dallas](https://cs.utdallas.edu/), advised by Prof. [Xiaohu Guo](https://personal.utdallas.edu/~xguo/), and my M.S. and B.S. from [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/), where I was advised by Prof. [Yu-shen Liu](https://yushen-liu.github.io/) and Prof. [Jun-hai Yong](https://www.thss.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/faculty/junhaiyong.htm).
